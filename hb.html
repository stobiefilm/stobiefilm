Director's Note

Hollywood Boulevard was truly an experiment of self confidence. Truth is I had a huge fear of being on screen. The challenge then to endeavor an otherwise group activity, as a solo project, was just exciting enough to sink my teeth into. It also helps I've never found it challenging to make myself look like a clown to make my mates laugh.
