South Australian.

The Stobie pole found in South Australia is a unique type of power line pole made from two steel I-beams held apart by a slab of concrete. It was invented by James Cyril Stobie in 1924 as a solution to the shortage of suitable timber for power poles in the region. The design allows for greater durability and resistance to environmental factors, making it a practical choice for supporting electrical infrastructure.
